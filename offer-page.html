The problem I couldn't stop seeing.

What I kept watching.

I have sat across from patients who cover their mouths when they laugh. Every week someone tells me the same story: they hate their smile, they have stopped being in photos with their kids — and the fix I can offer in my chair means drilling down healthy enamel and a bill between ten and twenty thousand dollars.

For most hardworking people, that is not an option.

So they buy a cheap online snap-on instead, and a few weeks later they are showing me what arrived: one mold made for nobody, a thick plastic gumline, identical piano-key teeth. Straight into a drawer.

I kept watching people choose between a smile they could not afford and a smile that fooled nobody.

I knew the details that make teeth look real could be engineered into a removable set if someone actually did the work.

So I did. Every Revive case is produced against the 7-Point Smile Design Protocol I built. If a case does not pass my standard, it does not ship.

One thing I want to be clear about: I am a cosmetic dentist, but I am not your dentist, and Revive is not dental treatment. I designed a product.

The Protocol

Seven checkpoints that make them look like real teeth, not a plastic cover.

I know which details the eye reads as natural and which ones give an appliance away instantly. Each checkpoint exists to get one of them exactly right for you. Tap any checkpoint and I will tell you what it controls.

01Smile Architecture

Mapped to your lip line and mouth width, not a template.

02Bite Alignment

Engineered so your upper and lower teeth meet correctly — comfortable for daily wear.

03Tooth-by-Tooth Proportions

Each tooth sized and shaped against its neighbors, with the slight variation that reads as natural.

04Gumline Precision

Contoured to your anatomy, eliminating the thick plastic line that gives cheap snap-ons away.

05Natural Translucency

Light passes through the edge like real enamel, not flat white plastic.

06Shade Matching

Bright but believable — matched to your complexion.

07Final Inspection

Checked against every point above, against your impressions, before it ships.

If it does not pass all seven, it does not leave the facility.

Questions I hear most often before someone orders.

What people want to know before they decide.

Should I get both or a single set?

Most people order both — when upper and lower are made from the same impressions, the result reads as consistent when you talk, laugh, or show up in photos. A single set is a perfectly good option if you only want to address one.

What happens after I order?

Your impression kit ships within 48 hours, and everything you need is already inside the box, including a prepaid return label so that sending your impressions back costs you nothing. Once we receive your impressions, production of your set begins.

Can I cancel after ordering?

Yes, you can cancel for a full refund at any point before production begins, because once we start building from your impressions, your set is already being made specifically and only for you, and that cannot be undone. Email support@reviveteeth.com and we will take care of everything from there.

How long do they last?

It is built for daily wear. Treat it like the custom appliance it is: remove them for hard or sticky foods, rinse them, store them in the case, and they stay looking the way they arrived. And if anything is wrong when your set arrives, we remake it free, up to twice.

Can I wear them while eating?

Yes, for everyday soft foods. Revive Veneers are made for daily wear, including eating and drinking. For hard, crunchy, sticky, or very hot foods, take them out first. They are a cosmetic appliance, not a replacement for your natural bite, so they are not built for heavy chewing. Removing them for tougher meals also helps them last longer.

What if they don't feel right?

Then we make it right. Your set was made to your mouth, so if it is off, that is our responsibility and not yours. We remake it free, up to twice, with prepaid shipping both ways, and a full refund is available any time before production begins. Email support@reviveteeth.com and we handle everything from there.
