What I kept watching.

I have sat across from patients who cover their mouths when they laugh. It is the hardest thing to watch as a dentist.

In my practice, I hear the same story every week. A patient comes in. They hate their smile. They have stopped being in photos with their kids. They want it fixed, but the clinical reality is brutal: traditional porcelain veneers mean hours in my chair, permanently drilling down healthy enamel, and a bill between ten and twenty thousand dollars.

For most hardworking people, that is not an option.

So they go online and buy a cheap plastic snap-on for a couple hundred dollars. A few weeks later, they are sitting in my chair again, showing me what arrived. It is always the same. The appliance is stamped from a single mold. The gingival margin, the gumline, is a straight, thick plastic ridge. The teeth look like identical white piano keys. They put it in, realize everyone can immediately tell it is fake, and throw it in a drawer.

I got tired of seeing it. I know exactly what makes a tooth look real. It is not magic. It is proportion, translucency, and how the edge meets the gum. I knew those clinical standards could be engineered into a removable appliance if someone actually did the work. So I did.

Every Revive case is produced against the 7-Point Smile Design Protocol I built. If a case does not pass my standard, it does not ship.

Let me be clear about one thing. I am a cosmetic dentist, but I am not your dentist, and Revive is not dental treatment. I designed a product.

The Protocol

Seven checkpoints that make them look like real teeth, not a plastic cover.

I know which details the human eye reads as natural and which ones it immediately reads as fake, and every point in this protocol exists to get one of those details exactly right for you before your set ever leaves the facility. Tap any checkpoint and I will tell you exactly what it controls.

01Smile Architecture

We map the design to your specific facial proportions, not a template. Your lip line and mouth width dictate the shape, ensuring the final result actually belongs on your face.

02Bite Alignment

If the appliance fights your natural bite, you will not wear it. We engineer the alignment so your upper and lower teeth meet correctly, making the set comfortable enough for daily use.

03Tooth-by-Tooth Proportions

Natural teeth are not identical tiles. We size and shape each tooth individually in proportion to the ones next to it. That slight, deliberate variation is what makes a smile look real.

04Gumline Precision

This is where cheap snap-ons fail. We contour the edge where the veneer meets your gum exactly to your anatomy. It eliminates the thick plastic line that instantly gives away a fake appliance.

05Natural Translucency

Real enamel is slightly see-through at the incisal edge (the biting edge). We replicate that translucency so your teeth catch light naturally, rather than looking like a solid block of white plastic.

06Shade Matching

The color must be bright but believable. We select a shade that looks natural for your complexion, avoiding the fluorescent white that tells everyone you are wearing something artificial.

07Final Inspection

Before anything ships, the finished case is checked against the six design decisions above. With this final inspection, that is seven checkpoints in total. If the case misses on any of them, it does not go out. That is the point of putting my name on it.

If it does not pass all seven, it does not leave the facility.

Questions I hear most often before someone orders.

What people want to know before they decide.

Should I get both or a single set?

Most people order both, and the reason is straightforward: when your upper and lower are produced from the same impressions, the result reads as consistent and natural when you talk, laugh, or appear in a photo. A single set is a perfectly good option if you only want to address one. Either way, your set is built from your own impressions and nobody else's.

What happens after I order?

Your impression kit ships within 48 hours, and everything you need is already inside the box, including a prepaid return label so that sending your impressions back costs you nothing. Once we receive your impressions, production of your set begins.

Can I cancel after ordering?

Yes, you can cancel for a full refund at any point before production begins, because once we start building from your impressions, your set is already being made specifically and only for you, and that cannot be undone. Email support@reviveteeth.com and we will take care of everything from there.

How long do they last?

How long your set holds up depends on how it is used and cared for, and I am not willing to make a durability promise that might not hold true for every person, because I think that kind of claim does you a disservice. What I will promise is two free remakes if something is not right when your set arrives, because that is a promise we can actually stand behind.

Can I wear them while eating?

Yes, for everyday soft foods. Revive Veneers are made for daily wear, including eating and drinking. For hard, crunchy, sticky, or very hot foods, take them out first. They are a cosmetic appliance, not a replacement for your natural bite, so they are not built for heavy chewing. Removing them for tougher meals also helps them last longer.

What if they don't feel right?

Then we make it right. Your set was made to your mouth, so if it is off, that is our responsibility and not yours. We remake it free, up to twice, with prepaid shipping both ways, and a full refund is available any time before production begins. Email support@reviveteeth.com and we handle everything from there.
